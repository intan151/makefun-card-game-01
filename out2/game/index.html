EMBER DUEL

A four-element card battler. You against the Rival — first ember to burn out loses.

▲ Fire ● Water ◆ Leaf ■ Stone

Fire beats Leaf · Leaf beats Stone · Stone beats Water · Water beats Fire (+2 damage).
Play two cards of one element in a turn for a chain bonus (+2). Energy grows each turn.

Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it — it costs energy and deals its attack as damage. Press Enter to end your turn; the Rival then plays. Both sides start at 30 life. Reduce the Rival to 0 to win. M toggles sound.
